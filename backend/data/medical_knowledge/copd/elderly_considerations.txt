COPD in Elderly Patients

Older adults with respiratory conditions may have additional health conditions and medications that should be considered when reviewing respiratory observations.

Caregiver monitoring should consider breathing, activity, mobility, sleep, oxygen observations when available, and changes from the patient's usual condition.

An AI system may support monitoring but should not replace professional diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
