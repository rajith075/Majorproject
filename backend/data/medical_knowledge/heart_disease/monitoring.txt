Heart Disease Monitoring

Monitoring should consider available cardiovascular observations together with the patient's usual baseline, activity, symptoms, medication adherence, and overall health status.

Caregivers should pay attention to persistent or meaningful changes in available health measurements or patient condition and communicate concerning changes to an appropriate healthcare professional.

An AI monitoring system may help identify patterns but should not determine clinical treatment.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
