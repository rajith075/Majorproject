Heart Disease in Elderly Patients

Older adults may have multiple health conditions and medications that provide important context when reviewing cardiovascular observations.

Caregiver monitoring should consider trends rather than isolated observations and should take into account mobility, activity, sleep, hydration, medication adherence, and changes from the patient's usual baseline.

An AI system can assist with monitoring and explanation but should not replace professional diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
