Diabetes Monitoring

Regular monitoring of blood glucose can help identify changes from a patient's usual readings and support ongoing health observation.

Caregivers should record available blood glucose measurements consistently and pay attention to repeated abnormal readings or meaningful changes from the patient's established baseline.

Blood glucose observations should be considered together with other available information, including symptoms, medication adherence, food intake, physical activity, hydration, sleep, and overall health status.

Persistent or concerning changes in blood glucose should be communicated to an appropriate healthcare professional for clinical assessment.

An AI monitoring system may help identify patterns in health data, but it should not be used to diagnose diabetes or determine treatment.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
