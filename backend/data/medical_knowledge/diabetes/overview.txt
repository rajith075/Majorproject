Diabetes in Older Adults

Diabetes is a chronic condition in which blood glucose levels remain higher than the normal range because the body does not produce enough insulin, does not use insulin effectively, or both.

In older adults, diabetes management should consider overall health, existing medical conditions, medications, nutrition, physical activity, and the individual's usual health status.

Blood glucose observations should be interpreted together with other available patient information rather than treated as a diagnosis by an AI monitoring system.

An AI monitoring system can help identify patterns and changes in available health data, but it should not replace professional diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
