Parkinson's Disease in Elderly Patients

Older adults with Parkinson's disease may experience changes in mobility, balance, daily activities, and sleep that should be considered together with their usual baseline.

Caregiver monitoring should consider mobility, fall risk observations, activity, medication adherence, sleep, and changes in daily functioning.

An AI system can assist with monitoring but should not replace professional assessment.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
