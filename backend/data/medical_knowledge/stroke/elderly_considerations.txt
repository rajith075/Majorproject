Stroke in Elderly Patients

Older adults may have changes in mobility, memory, balance, communication, or other functions that require comparison with their established baseline.

Caregiver monitoring should focus on meaningful changes from the patient's usual condition and should consider existing medical conditions, medications, mobility, and overall health.

An AI monitoring system can help identify patterns but should not replace professional clinical assessment.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
