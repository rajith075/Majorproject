Cancer in Elderly Patients

Older adults with cancer may have additional health conditions, medications, and functional considerations that provide important context.

Caregiver monitoring should consider nutrition, hydration, mobility, activity, sleep, symptoms, medication adherence, and changes from the patient's baseline.

An AI monitoring system can assist with organizing observations but should not replace professional clinical decisions.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
