Hypertension in Older Adults

Blood pressure monitoring is particularly relevant in older adults because cardiovascular risk can be influenced by age, existing medical conditions, medications, lifestyle factors, and changes from the individual's usual baseline.

Caregiver monitoring should focus on trends rather than isolated readings. Repeated elevated blood pressure measurements, significant changes from baseline, or new symptoms should be communicated to an appropriate healthcare professional.

The patient's overall health, mobility, medication adherence, hydration, activity, sleep, and other relevant factors may provide additional context when reviewing blood pressure observations.

An AI monitoring system can help identify patterns and provide explanations, but it should not be used as a substitute for professional diagnosis or clinical decision-making.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
