

Hypertension refers to persistently elevated blood pressure. It is an important cardiovascular risk factor and may increase the risk of complications involving the heart, brain, kidneys, and blood vessels.

Blood pressure assessment should consider repeated measurements rather than relying on a single reading. Interpretation should also consider the individual's usual baseline, overall health, existing conditions, medications, and other relevant risk factors.

For an elderly-care monitoring system, an elevated reading should be interpreted together with the patient's history and other observations rather than treated as a diagnosis by the AI system.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
