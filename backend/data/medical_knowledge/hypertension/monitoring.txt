Hypertension Monitoring

Regular blood pressure monitoring can help identify persistent changes from a patient's usual readings.

Caregivers should record blood pressure measurements consistently and pay attention to repeated elevated readings or meaningful changes from the patient's established baseline.

Blood pressure observations should be considered together with other available information, including heart rate, symptoms, existing medical conditions, medication adherence, activity, sleep, and overall health status.

A healthcare professional should evaluate persistent or concerning changes and determine appropriate clinical management.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
