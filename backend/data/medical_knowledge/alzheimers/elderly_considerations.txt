Alzheimer's Disease in Elderly Patients

Older adults with cognitive impairment may require monitoring of changes in memory, behavior, daily activities, mobility, sleep, hydration, and overall functioning.

Caregiver observations should focus on changes from the patient's established baseline rather than isolated observations.

An AI system may support pattern recognition but should not replace professional diagnosis or care decisions.

This information is provided for health monitoring and educational purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.
